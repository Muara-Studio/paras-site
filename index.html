FILE 01 // ABOUT

PARAS Portrait Analysis, Reconstruction & Automated Sorting

A camera finds your face, maps it, cuts it into pieces, files the pieces and tries to put you back together. The more it sorts, the less the result looks like you.

Paras is Indonesian for a face, the way people see it. This piece is a Muara Studio study of IRIS, Antikode's installation. All tracking runs in your browser; no frame is stored or sent.
